Proposal: integrate CRM PLUS through the Oshinei CRM API, with Buzzebees as the lead party and no change to the Easy POS application

Thank you for the CRM PLUS POS API specification. Your API requires the POS application to call Buzzebees at checkout, which would require development by Easy POS. We propose a model that needs no change to the POS application and no access by any third party to our POS database. Oshinei already reads completed sales from the Easy POS head-office database (HQ_Oshinei) into its own analytics application with a read-only account. We will add a CRM module to that application and publish an HTTPS API: Buzzebees reads bills from it, runs point earning, reversal and coupon tracking in CRM PLUS, and writes every result back to it. This letter sets out the model, the evidence from our sales data, the API specification, and what we ask of Buzzebees and Easy POS.

1. Summary

Model. Buzzebees is the lead party and builds a connector to the Oshinei CRM API. Every 5 minutes it reads bills that are new or changed, including voids. Members identify a bill by claiming the receipt in the LINE Official Account. Buzzebees credits points, reverses them when a bill is voided on any later day, marks coupons used when a cashier enters the code in Easy's existing "external coupon" function, and reports each of these results back to Oshinei through the API.

What changes. Oshinei builds and operates the API (section 4). Easy POS develops nothing and grants no database access; it only confirms how its coupon and receipt functions work (section 7).

Trade-off. Points arrive after the bill reaches the head office and the member claims it, not at the counter, and coupons are checked after use rather than before. Section 2.2 compares this with the earlier options.

2. Proposed integration model

Sales flow from branch POS terminals to the head-office database as they do today. Oshinei's application reads them with its existing read-only account and publishes them through the CRM API. Buzzebees reads from the API, runs all loyalty logic on its own platform, and writes the results back. No party other than Oshinei connects to the POS database.

4. Oshinei CRM API specification

Version 1, proposed for the pilot. Field names and paths are final unless Buzzebees asks for a change in its reply. Oshinei builds the source of every field from HQ_Oshinei with its existing read-only account.

4.1 Conventions

ItemRule

TransportTLS 1.2 or later. Requests are accepted only from the Buzzebees IP addresses on the allow-list
AuthenticationHeader Authorization: Bearer <token>. Two tokens: one with read scope and one with write scope. Tokens never appear in URLs or logs and are rotated every 90 days

4.7 PUT /v1/daily-summary/{date} and GET /v1/reconciliation/{date}

By 06:00 each day Buzzebees sends the totals for the previous business date: claims accepted, held and rejected; points credited and reversed; coupons issued, used, restored and used twice; new members and total members. Sending the summary again replaces it, and Oshinei keeps every version.

Oshinei then compares the summary with the events received and with sales in the bill feed. /v1/reconciliation/{date} returns matched or mismatch with a list of differences (item, Buzzebees value, Oshinei value). Differences are resolved within 3 business days (R7).

5. Process flows

5.1 Earning by receipt claim

Figure 5. Receipt claim. If the bill has not reached the API yet, the claim is held and re-checked automatically for up to 48 hours.
  1. The customer pays as today. The receipt shows the branch and receipt number (SaleNum).
  2. The bill reaches HQ_Oshinei through Easy's existing branch upload, Oshinei's feed reads it, and it appears in /v1/bills/changes.
  3. In the LINE Official Account the member enters the branch, receipt number and amount paid, or uploads a photo of the receipt.
  4. Buzzebees checks that the bill exists, is paid, matches the amount, is within the claim window and has not been claimed. It may call /v1/bills/{bill_key} to check at once. If the bill is not there yet, the claim is held and re-checked for up to 48 hours.
  5. CRM PLUS credits the points according to the earn rules (D2).
  6. The connector writes claim.accepted and points.credited to /v1/events.
  7. The member receives a confirmation in LINE.

5.2 Reversal when a bill is voided

Figure 6. Reversal timeline. In 2026 the latest void was 19 days after the sale; the change feed delivers it whenever it happens.

If the member has already spent the points, CRM PLUS applies the rule chosen by Oshinei (D4): a negative balance, or deduction from the next credit. A bill that becomes returned is treated the same way.

5.3 Coupon lifecycle

Figure 7. Coupon lifecycle. Each step is reported to Oshinei as an event.
  1. CRM PLUS issues a coupon with a unique number, from a campaign or in exchange for points (R5).
  2. The member shows the coupon in LINE. The cashier applies it in Easy as an external coupon of the Buzzebees series and types the number.
  3. The bill reaches the API with the coupon in coupons. The connector marks the coupon used in CRM PLUS and writes coupon.used.
  4. A second use of the same number is not blocked at the counter. The connector writes coupon.duplicate_use; the Oshinei CRM module shows it to head office the same day with the branch and bill.
  5. If the bill is voided, the connector restores the coupon and writes coupon.restored.

Because use is checked after the event, we will limit Buzzebees coupons to modest values during the pilot (D5) and ask Easy whether the coupon series can block reuse at the counter (E1).

WhySaleNum is sequential within a day (section 3.3), so a receipt number alone could be guessed. The amount match and daily limit prevent claiming other customers' bills.

10. Pilot, acceptance tests and next steps

10.1 Acceptance tests (pilot branch)

  • A paid bill claimed with the correct amount earns points once; a second claim of the same bill is rejected.
  • A claim with a wrong amount, an expired date or an unknown receipt is rejected with a clear reason.
  • A claim made before the bill reaches the API is held and credited automatically once the bill arrives.
  • A claimed bill voided on day 0 and another voided on day 3 are both reversed within one polling cycle.
  • A coupon used once is marked used; a second use of the same code is reported the same day; a coupon on a voided bill is restored.
  • Reading the change feed from a saved cursor twice causes no duplicate credit or reversal.
  • Sending the same events twice stores them once.
  • After a planned API outage of one hour, the connector catches up from its cursor and resends its events with nothing lost.
  • The reconciliation for each day of the test week shows matched.
