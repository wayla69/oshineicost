Proposal: integrate CRM PLUS through Oshinei's sales database, with Buzzebees as the lead party and no change to the Easy POS application

Thank you for the CRM PLUS POS API specification. Your API requires the POS application to call Buzzebees at checkout, which would require development by Easy POS. We propose a different model that needs no change to the POS application: Buzzebees reads completed sales from our Easy POS head-office database (HQ_Oshinei, Microsoft SQL Server) through read-only views, and runs point earning, reversal and coupon tracking on its own platform. This letter sets out the model, the evidence from our database, the data interface we will provide, and what we ask of Buzzebees and Easy POS.

1. Summary

Model. Buzzebees is the lead party and builds a connector that reads four read-only views in HQ_Oshinei every few minutes. Members identify a bill by claiming the receipt in the LINE Official Account. Buzzebees credits points, reverses them when a bill is voided on any later day, and marks coupons used when a cashier enters the code in Easy's existing "external coupon" function.

What changes for Easy POS. No application development. Easy is asked only for configuration: a read-only database login and views, network access, and a coupon series for Buzzebees codes (section 7).

Trade-off. Points arrive after the bill reaches the head-office database, not at the counter, and coupons are checked after use rather than before. Section 2.2 compares this with the real-time model.

2. Proposed integration model

Sales flow from branch POS terminals to the head-office database as they do today. Buzzebees reads them from four read-only views and runs all loyalty logic on its own platform. The only link back to the shop is the coupon code a cashier types into Easy.

Implication for polling. At the busiest hour, a 5-minute poll returns about 40 new or changed bills. The load on the database is small if the connector reads only changed rows (section 4.3).

3.2 No loyalty data exists today

No bill in 2026 carries points (Sale.EarnPoint and Sale.RedeemedPoint are zero on all 500,905 bills and on all bills of 1–26 October). The Easy membership tables (MemberCard, CustPoint, CustomerLoyalty, LoyaltyRank and others) are empty. The Customer table (7,128 rows) holds tax-invoice records, not members. The member base therefore starts in CRM PLUS with no migration.

3.3 How a bill is identified

In September 2026 the 49,665 finalised bills had 49,665 unique branch + SaleNum pairs but only 33,542 distinct SaleId values. The bill key in this proposal is therefore branch code + SaleNum. SaleNum is printed on the receipt, contains the business date and a daily sequence, and is predictable; section 6 explains why a claim must also match the amount.

Figure 3. Bill identifier in Easy POS, used as the claim and credit key in this proposal.

3.4 Voids happen up to 19 days after the sale

Of 2,482 voided bills, 2,446 (98.5%) were voided on the day of sale and 36 on a later day, up to 19 days after. Every voided bill updates Sale.UpdateDate to the void time or later (2,481 of 2,482), so the connector finds voids by reading changed rows. A credit given on day 0 can therefore always be reversed, unlike the same-day limit of /earn/cancel.

Figure 4. Voids made after the day of sale, by delay in days. Total 36; the longest delay was 19 days.

3.5 Data reaches the head office during the day

Easy records each branch's last upload in Branch.LastSync. On 27 October 2026 at 11:17, 16 of 31 active branches had uploaded within the previous 30 minutes, 14 within the previous 24 hours, and 1 had not uploaded for more than a day. A bill closed at 10:38 was already readable at 11:13. Upload timing differs by branch, so we will measure it for one week during the pilot and agree a target with Easy (E6). The connector should alert when a branch has not uploaded for 3 hours during trading (R7).

3.6 External coupons are already used at the counter

Cashiers already enter external coupon numbers into Easy. They are stored in CouponCardOutsideSystemUsed with the coupon series, coupon number, bill, branch, discount and time. From January to September 2026 there were 38,156 uses of 20,459 distinct coupon numbers. 280 numbers were used on more than one bill (4,506 uses in total), because only 3 of Easy's 136 coupon series enforce unique numbers (IsUniqueCouponNumber). On 26 October 2026, coupon number 2499 of one series was used on two bills in the same branch 77 minutes apart. Buzzebees codes must therefore be unique and checked after use (R4).

4.3 Access and polling rules

  • Account: one SQL Server login for Buzzebees with SELECT on schema bzb only. No access to base tables, no write, no stored procedures.
  • Network: encrypted connection (TLS) from fixed Buzzebees IP addresses on an allow-list, or through a site-to-site VPN; the exact method to be agreed with Easy (E2).
  • Incremental read: every 5 minutes, read rows with updated_at later than the last watermark minus 10 minutes, to catch rows committed late. Keys are idempotent, so rows read twice cause no double credit.
  • Nightly check: once a night, between 02:00 and 05:00, re-read the last 35 days to catch any change missed during the day.
  • Limits: one connection at a time; queries must filter on updated_at or business_date; no full scans during trading hours (10:00–22:00).
  • No writes: Buzzebees never writes to HQ_Oshinei. Results stay in CRM PLUS and reach Oshinei through the daily report.

5. Process flows

5.1 Earning by receipt claim

Figure 5. Receipt claim. If the bill has not reached HQ yet, the claim is held and re-checked automatically for up to 48 hours.
  1. The customer pays as today. The receipt shows the branch and receipt number (SaleNum).
  2. The bill reaches HQ_Oshinei through Easy's existing branch upload and appears in bzb.v_bill.
  3. In the LINE Official Account the member enters the branch, receipt number and amount paid, or uploads a photo of the receipt.
  4. Buzzebees checks that the bill exists, is paid, matches the amount, is within the claim window and has not been claimed. If the bill is not yet in HQ, the claim is held and re-checked for up to 48 hours.
  5. CRM PLUS credits the points according to the earn rules (D2) and records the bill key against the credit.
  6. The member receives a confirmation in LINE.

5.2 Reversal when a bill is voided

Figure 6. Reversal timeline. In 2026 the latest void was 19 days after the sale; the nightly 35-day re-check covers this.

If the member has already spent the points, CRM PLUS applies the rule chosen by Oshinei (D4): a negative balance, or deduction from the next credit. A bill that becomes return is treated the same way.

5.3 Coupon lifecycle

Figure 7. Coupon lifecycle in the database model.
  1. CRM PLUS issues a coupon with a unique number, from a campaign or in exchange for points (R5).
  2. The member shows the coupon in LINE. The cashier applies it in Easy as an external coupon of the Buzzebees series and types the number.
  3. The connector reads the use from bzb.v_coupon_use and marks the coupon used in CRM PLUS (for example by calling /wallet/pay with to_use=true from the back end).
  4. A second use of the same number is not blocked at the counter, so it is reported in the next daily report with the branch and bill. Oshinei follows it up with the branch.
  5. If the bill is voided, the connector restores the coupon to unused.

Because use is checked after the event, we will limit Buzzebees coupons to modest values during the pilot (D5) and ask Easy whether the coupon series can enforce unique numbers at the counter (E3).

6. Requests to Buzzebees (R1–R10)

R1 — Build and operate the database connector Blocking

RequestConfirm that Buzzebees will build, host and operate the connector that reads the bzb views and credits, reverses and marks coupons in CRM PLUS. Please state the CRM PLUS functions it will use (Appendix A), the effort, the cost and the delivery date.
WhyThis makes Buzzebees the single lead party and removes POS development by Easy.

R2 — Receipt claim in the LINE Official Account Blocking

RequestA claim screen that accepts branch, receipt number and amount paid, or a receipt photo. A claim succeeds only if all of the following hold:
• the bill key exists in v_bill with status paid;
• the amount matches total within 1 THB;
• the business date is within the claim window (7 days proposed, D3);
• the bill key has not been claimed before;
• the member has made no more than 5 claims that day (proposed).
Claims for bills not yet in HQ are held and re-checked for up to 48 hours.
WhySaleNum is sequential within a day (section 3.3), so a receipt number alone could be guessed. The amount match and daily limit prevent claiming other customers' bills.

R3 — Automatic reversal on void Blocking

RequestWhen a claimed bill changes to voided or return, reverse the credit within one polling cycle, on any day, and notify the member. Re-reading the same change must not reverse twice.
Why36 bills in 2026 were voided after the day of sale, up to 19 days later (section 3.4). /earn/cancel in your specification allows same-day reversal only.

R4 — Coupon codes for Easy external coupons Blocking

RequestIssue coupons with unique numeric codes of a fixed length that a cashier can type into Easy (length to be agreed with Easy, E3). Mark a coupon used when it appears in v_coupon_use; restore it when the bill is voided; report every second use of a code with branch, bill and time.
Why280 external coupon numbers were used on more than one bill in 2026, because Easy enforces unique numbers in only 3 of 136 series (section 3.6).

R5 — Exchanging points for coupons in LINE Before pilot

RequestMembers burn points only in LINE, by exchanging them for a coupon (R4). No point burning at the counter. Please confirm the exchange catalogue is configurable by Oshinei.

R6 — Polling discipline Before pilot

RequestFollow section 4.3: SELECT on bzb views only, incremental reads every 5 minutes, a nightly 35-day check outside trading hours, one connection, fixed source IP addresses. Provide the IP addresses and a technical contact for incidents.

R7 — Daily report and alerts Before pilot

10. Pilot, acceptance tests and next steps

10.1 Acceptance tests (pilot branch)

  • A paid bill claimed with the correct amount earns points once; a second claim of the same bill is rejected.
  • A claim with a wrong amount, an expired date or an unknown receipt is rejected with a clear reason.
  • A claim made before the bill reaches HQ is held and credited automatically once the bill arrives.
  • A claimed bill voided on day 0 and another voided on day 3 are both reversed within one polling cycle.
  • A coupon used once is marked used; a second use of the same code appears in the next daily report; a coupon on a voided bill is restored.
  • Re-reading the same rows (watermark overlap and nightly check) causes no duplicate credit or reversal.
  • The daily report for the test week matches v_bill and v_coupon_use exactly.
  • A branch with no upload for 3 hours during trading raises an alert.
