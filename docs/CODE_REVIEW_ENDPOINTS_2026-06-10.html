🎯 Headline: known "GP day/week 2× scaling" — ROOT CAUSE FOUND

✅ Root cause located in /api/analysis/gross_profit (line ~5566), NOT in /api/buffet/gp or /api/gp/by-branch-month

The daily/weekly path prorates the full month's revenue & COGS onto days, but the share denominator (bm_daily_total_inc) is summed only over days inside the selected date window. Pick half a month → every day shows ~2× revenue, COGS and GP. Exact 2× when the window is half the month; correct only for whole-month windows. See finding C1.

Cleared: /api/buffet/gp was executed empirically — month, day, and week granularity all return identical period GP (52,090,233 for 2026-04); its pro-ration conserves revenue and cost. /api/gp/by-branch-month fractions were verified to sum to exactly 1 per branch-month.

🧩 Cross-cutting themes (same bug, many endpoints)

1. group (master/franchise) filter silently dropped in sub-queries

Several endpoints apply _group_filter to the main query but not to companion COGS/MoM/sparkline queries — group-filtered views mix group revenue with whole-chain COGS. Findings A1, A2, A3, A4.

2. receipt_no joined without branch/date scope

receipt_no is NOT globally unique (per-branch counter; verified 406 values exist in >1 branch; unscoped join already yields 8 phantom rows). Joins on receipt_no alone mispair one branch's revenue with another's COGS, and sale_bill_cogs's PK = receipt_no means colliding bills silently overwrite at sync time. Findings A5, B2, F3, D4, D9.

3. avg_ticket is inc-VAT while revenue on the same row is ex-VAT

AVG(total_inc_vat) without /1.07 in at least 9 endpoints, while /api/analysis/persona and /api/profit/summary correctly de-VAT — the same KPI differs by exactly 7% across tabs. Findings D6, E5, C9, F5. One shared fix: change every AVG(st.total_inc_vat) to AVG(st.total_inc_vat/1.07).

4. BoM-first unified COGS vs legacy sku_cost-only gating

COGS now resolves BoM-recipe-first (_unified_cogs_expr), but several endpoints still gate "has cost" / "costed revenue" / "uncosted count" on the old sku_cost chain. 98 BoM-costed SKUs (carrying ฿333M = 54% of total COGS) fall through the cracks. Findings A6, E3, E7, F6, G3, G4, G7.

5. Partial in-progress month treated as a full data point

The live month (synced daily) enters trend/forecast/health/lifecycle math as if complete, producing fake collapses early in each month. Findings D7, D8, C8, G5, G6.

6. Hardcoded buffet tier SKU codes that don't exist in the DB

Code references BUFFET590/BUFFET799/B01/B02/B03/B07/B11 — none exist; the real SKUs are plain BUFFET and BUFFETCHILD. Several buffet features are therefore permanently no-op or 100%-wrong. Findings C3, C4, C5.

🔴 HIGH severity (14)

HIGH C1 — GP 2× at day/week: window-only share denominator vs full-month numerator

/api/analysis/gross_profit · main.py:5566 · confidence: high · ✔ verified in code

Daily/weekly proration computes share = day_rev_inc / bm_daily_total_inc[k] where the denominator sums only days inside the requested date_from/date_to window, but bm["cogs"]/bm["rev_ex"] are the FULL month's totals. Shares sum to 1 over the window, squeezing the whole month's revenue and COGS into the selected days.

Impact: Day/week GP, revenue and COGS roughly 2× too high for half-month windows (scales with window fraction). This is the documented "gp day/week 2× scaling" open issue. Fix: sum the share denominator over the full month (unfiltered by date), or scale bm totals by the in-window revenue fraction.

HIGH C2 — Buffet GP endpoints report 100% margin (COGS = 0)

/api/buffet/breakeven + tier-mix, upgrade-simulator, branch-tier-matrix, child-trend · main.py:5042 / 4092 / 4502 / 4837 / 4943 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

These endpoints filter to category BUFFET then aggregate _cogs_case() — but the unified expression deliberately returns 0 for tier SKUs (consumption is booked on item-level B0xxx rows in OTHER categories, which the filter excludes). Verified 2026-05: BUFFET category rev_ex = ฿110.4M, COGS = 0. The gp-calendar comment (lines 4744-4748) documents this exact trap, but only gp-calendar was fixed.

Impact: Every buffet GP/breakeven figure shows ~100% margin; breakeven's avg_gp_per_cover ≈ ฿705 instead of a realistic ~฿300, so required covers/day are understated ~2.4×.

HIGH F2 — Cost-sensitivity baseline GP is −13.4M when real GP is +52.1M

/api/buffet/cost-sensitivity · main.py:13017 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

Revenue excludes buffet tier SKUs (where ฿96M of ฿112M revenue is booked) while cost sums only the top-N (default 50) rows (฿29.1M of ฿59.7M actual). Two mismatched denominators → base_gp = −13,359,284 for 2026-04 vs the real +52,090,233 from /api/buffet/gp.

Impact: Every simulated margin-impact figure in the cost-sensitivity tool starts from a wildly wrong negative baseline.

HIGH F1 — buffet_mix counts buffet FOOD pieces and penalty fees as price-tier covers

/api/analysis/buffet_mix · main.py:11717 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

classify() tests tier substrings ('350'/'500'/'590'/'799') BEFORE the buffet-charge / rev>0 check. Zero-revenue (B) item SKUs whose names contain a tier number — e.g. B0068 "(B) ข้าวปั้นหน้าปลาไหล 590 (1 ชิ้น)", qty 153,629 — are counted as covers. Penalty SKUs ("ค่าปรับบุฟเฟ่ต์ 590++"), gift vouchers "500" and soju combos also land in tiers.

Impact: ~260k phantom "covers" dumped into 590/799 tiers (vs ~1.58M real covers); tier revenue share and the monthly trend chart polluted. Likely also a contributor to the known "89% untiered" anomaly reading strangely.

HIGH C3 — Upgrade simulator is permanently a no-op

/api/buffet/upgrade-simulator · main.py:4592 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

_tier_label can only produce '590'/'799' from SKU codes B02/B11 or names containing those numbers — but the BUFFET category contains exactly two SKUs: BUFFET and BUFFETCHILD. The if tier_590 and tier_799 branch never executes; every call returns delta_gp=0 with the note "tier data not found".

Impact: The what-if simulator silently never simulates anything, for any input.

HIGH C4 — Child-trend shows 100% children every month

/api/buffet/child-trend · main.py:4639 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

_BUFFET_ADULT_CODES lists only legacy codes (BUFFET350/500/590/799, B01…B11) — none exist in the DB. The actual adult SKU BUFFET (156,580 pax in 2026-05) matches neither adult nor child, so it is dropped; only BUFFETCHILD survives.

HIGH A1 — cost-per-head: COGS/revenue ignore the group filter

/api/sales/cost-per-head · main.py:2291 · confidence: high

The customers query applies _group_filter, but the COGS/revenue query over sales_sku_daily filters only by branch name. With ?group=master|franchise, customers are group-scoped while COGS/revenue cover all branches.

Impact: Group-filtered views divide all-chain COGS by group-only customers — cost_per_head, cost_pct and gp_per_head materially wrong; out-of-group branches appear with customers=0.

HIGH A2 — MTD headline: COGS ignores group while revenue respects it

/api/overall/latest-month-mtd · main.py:2368 · confidence: high

Same copy-paste pattern as A1: revenue/customers/discount from sales_transaction are group-filtered; the COGS aggregate over sales_sku_daily is not.

Impact: gross_profit = group revenue − whole-chain COGS → deeply negative/garbage GP and MoM on group-filtered MTD headlines.

HIGH B1 — /api/summary fallback divides ex-VAT revenue by 1.07 again

/api/summary · main.py:3439-3441 · confidence: high · ✔ verified in code + ingest rule

For branch×months missing from sales_sku_monthly, the fallback treats sales_month_summary.net_sales as inc-VAT and calls ex_vat() on it — but the ingest hard rule (ingest_easy.py:632) writes net_sales VAT-EXCLUSIVE, and other endpoints use it directly as ex-VAT.

Impact: Headline revenue and avg_ticket ~6.5% too low for fallback months; revenue_inc_vat is fed an ex-VAT figure, breaking POS reconciliation.

HIGH D1 — Discounts tab uses the broken subtotal base everywhere

/api/analysis/discounts · main.py:6241 · confidence: high

by_branch, top_cashiers, pattern_groups and overall gross_sales compute discount % against food+drink+other subtotal — a base that excludes the buffet per-head charge. The codebase itself documents the defect in discount_bills (lines 6383-6390: 221 bills read >100% discount) and fixed it there with (total_inc_vat − service)/1.07 + discount, but this endpoint was never migrated. The cashier outlier flag is computed from the inflated values.

Impact: Branch/cashier discount % wildly overstated on buffet bills; normal buffet bills classified "100% free"; wrong cashiers flagged for fraud review.

HIGH D2 — discount_composition buckets copy the same broken base

/api/analysis/discount_composition · main.py:6544 · confidence: high

The bucket CASE classifies bills by st.discount vs subtotal gross — same defect as D1; a 10% discount on a buffet bill lands in 'free' or 'ge50'. The correct base_expr already exists in discount_leakage in the very next endpoint.

Impact: Branches shown doing heavy 50%+/free discounting when they actually give small markdowns on buffet bills; per-branch "dominant bucket" wrong.

HIGH G1 — GP Excel export: every percentage 100× too small

/api/buffet/gp/export · main.py:13706/13742/13755/13768 · confidence: high · ✔ verified in code

PCT_FMT = '0.0"%"' uses a QUOTED % — a literal suffix with no ×100 scaling — yet every percent value is divided by 100 before writing ((s["margin_pct"] or 0) / 100). A 42.5% margin renders as "0.4%". The monthly-pack renderer (line 14082) uses the same literal format with UNdivided values, which is correct.

Impact: Margin %, vs-network pp, and % of total cost in the downloaded Excel are wrong by a factor of 100 vs the dashboard. Fix: stop dividing by 100 (3 places).

HIGH G3 — Recalibration worklist hides 54% of total COGS

/api/cost/recalibration · main.py:15102 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

The per-SKU query INNER JOINs sku_cost and filters on the legacy effective-cost chain, but total COGS uses the unified BoM-first expression. 98 BoM-only SKUs carry ฿333.1M of ฿615.1M total COGS (54.2%) — mostly buffet B0xxx items, the true top drivers — and can never appear in the list, while pct_of_total_cogs uses the full denominator.

Impact: The worklist shows only the sku_cost-priced minority (mostly bogus price-as-cost rows per the 2026-06-01 audit) and hides the SKUs that drive over half of food cost.

HIGH G5 — Revenue forecast OLS includes the partial live month

/api/analysis/revenue-forecast · main.py:13626 · confidence: high · ✔ verified vs live DB

History GROUPs BY period_month with no live-month guard. Verified today: 2026-06 = ฿29.6M (day 10) vs ฿126.7M (May). The crash point drags the OLS slope strongly negative, inflates residual sd (huge confidence band), and poisons the first forecast bucket.

Impact: For most of every month the forecast projects collapse; r_squared/trend stats meaningless.
